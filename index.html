Our process

From First Measurement to Ongoing Service

Five steps we follow on every job, whether it is a single hood or a full kitchen exhaust system.

01

Understand

We look at the kitchen or the site, take measurements and understand what the exhaust actually has to handle.

02

Recommend

We suggest the hood, fan or blower that suits the load and the discharge route, with the reasoning behind it.

03

Manufacture / Supply

Ducting and hoods are fabricated in our workshop; bought-out units are supplied to size.

04

Fit & Install

The route is set out, sections are fitted and supported, and the unit is installed and run.

05

Service

Cleaning, checks and repairs keep the system working the way it did on day one.

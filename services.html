01 — Service

Chimney Sales

We supply commercial kitchen chimneys, canopy hoods, baffle filters, axial and centrifugal exhaust units and the fittings that go with them. Sizing is worked out from your kitchen layout, the equipment under the hood and the discharge route, so what you buy matches the job rather than a catalogue number.

Key benefits

  • Sized for your kitchen and equipment load
  • Stainless steel and galvanised options
  • Hoods, filters, fans, blowers and accessories
  • Guidance on the right unit before you buy

Suitable for

  • Hotels and restaurants
  • Bakeries and sweet stalls
  • Canteens and messes
  • Small industrial units

02 — Service

Chimney Manufacturing

Ducting is fabricated in our workshop from stainless steel and galvanised sheet — straight runs, radius elbows, square-to-round transitions, weather cowls and canopy hoods, all cut and formed to the dimensions taken from your site. Making the parts ourselves means an awkward corner or a low ceiling is handled at the fabrication stage instead of on site.

Key benefits

  • Made to site measurements, not stock sizes
  • Stainless steel and galvanised sheet
  • Radius elbows, transitions, cowls and hoods
  • Awkward layouts handled in fabrication

Suitable for

  • Custom canopy hoods
  • Duct runs and risers
  • Discharge cowls and weather hoods
  • Replacement sections

03 — Service

Chimney Fittings

Fitting is where an exhaust system is made or lost. We set out the duct route, fabricate and fit the sections, support them properly off the slab or wall, seal the joints and terminate the run cleanly. The route is planned so filters, access panels and the motor stay reachable once the kitchen is running.

Key benefits

  • Planned duct route before work begins
  • Properly supported and sealed joints
  • Access kept for later cleaning
  • Neat finish in visible areas

Suitable for

  • Overhead duct routing
  • Vertical risers and shafts
  • External wall runs
  • Hood-to-duct connections

04 — Service

Chimney Installation

We handle the full installation: mounting the hood at the right height over the range, running the duct, setting the blower or exhaust fan on its base, wiring it to your point and terminating the discharge above roof level. Work is planned around your kitchen so a running business loses as little time as possible.

Key benefits

  • Hood set at the correct working height
  • Blower mounted on a proper base
  • Discharge terminated above roof level
  • Planned around your kitchen hours

Suitable for

  • New commercial kitchens
  • Kitchen expansions
  • Rooftop blower installation
  • Exhaust system replacement

05 — Service

Chimney Servicing

Grease build-up in the hood, filters and duct is what quietly kills suction and raises the fire risk in a commercial kitchen. Servicing covers cleaning the hood and baffle filters, clearing the duct run, checking the blower, belt and bearings, and setting the unit back to work properly.

Key benefits

  • Hood, filters and duct cleaned
  • Blower, belt and bearings checked
  • Suction restored across the line
  • Lower grease and fire risk

Suitable for

  • Hotels and restaurants
  • Industrial canteens
  • Scheduled periodic cleaning
  • Pre-inspection clean-ups
Enquire About Servicing WhatsApp
06 — Service

Repair & Maintenance

Weak suction, noise, vibration or a motor that trips usually has a specific cause — a choked duct, a slipping belt, a worn bearing, an undersized fan or a badly routed run. We look at the whole system rather than swapping parts, repair or replace what has failed, and tell you plainly if the design itself is the problem.

Key benefits

  • Fault traced across the whole system
  • Motor, belt and bearing replacement
  • Duct repair and re-routing
  • Works on systems we did not install

Suitable for

  • Weak or uneven suction
  • Noise and vibration
  • Damaged or leaking ducts
  • Motor and blower faults
Enquire About Repair & Maintenance WhatsApp
07 — Service

Custom Chimney Solutions

Not every kitchen or shed fits a standard solution. Low slabs, shared walls, long horizontal runs, multiple hoods on one discharge, or an exhaust requirement outside a kitchen altogether — these are worked out from site measurements, fabricated to suit and fitted as one system.

Key benefits

  • Site survey and measurement first
  • Fabrication built around the constraint
  • Multiple hoods on a single discharge
  • Industrial and non-kitchen exhaust

Suitable for

  • Tight or low-ceiling kitchens
  • Multi-hood layouts
  • Long external duct runs
  • Industrial exhaust and ventilation

08 — Service

Duct Fresh Air & Fresh Air Fan Systems

Pulling stale air out is only half the job — a kitchen also needs clean air coming back in. We manufacture and fit fresh air ducting, fresh air fans and filter units, so replacement air reaches the cooking line instead of being dragged in through doors and windows.

What we handle

  • Fresh air duct fabrication and routing
  • Fresh air fan supply and fitting
  • Fresh air filter units and grilles
  • Balanced supply against exhaust load

Suitable for

  • Enclosed and basement kitchens
  • Hotels, canteens and restaurants
  • Kitchens with heavy exhaust load
  • Workshops and production halls
